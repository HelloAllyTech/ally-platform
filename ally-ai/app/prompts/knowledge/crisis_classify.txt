A mental healthcare worker sent the message below to an automated question-answering assistant. Decide whether it is about a crisis happening now.

# Message
{message}

# What you are deciding
Return `is_crisis: true` when the sender, or a client they describe, is at risk of suicide, self-harm or harm right now, or someone's safety depends on what happens soon. Return `is_crisis: false` for reference questions asked in general terms, past cases, hypotheticals, and ordinary work stress. When unsure, choose `true`. Judge the message in whatever language it is written.

# What to return
- `is_crisis`: your decision.
- `signal`: the shortest phrase from the message that drove it, copied verbatim; empty when `is_crisis` is false.
- `confidence`: 0 to 1.
