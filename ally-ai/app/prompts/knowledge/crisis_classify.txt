Someone sent the message below to an automated question-answering assistant. The sender may be a mental healthcare worker, a counsellor, a trainee, or a member of the public. Decide whether the message shows that someone may be in danger now.

# Message
{message}

# What you are deciding
Return `is_crisis: true` when the message shows that the sender, or a person they describe, may be at risk of suicide, self-harm, harm to others or abuse right now, or is in acute distress — including indirect ways of saying it ("I can't keep doing this", "everyone would be better off without me", "she said she won't be here tomorrow").

Return `is_crisis: false` for messages with no sign of risk or distress: questions about a product, an app, an account, sign-in, features, settings, data, privacy, costs or contact details; greetings; reference questions asked in general terms; past cases; hypotheticals; ordinary work stress.

When a message contains a sign of risk or distress and you are unsure how serious it is, choose `true`. Do not choose `true` for a message that contains no such sign.

Judge the message in whatever language it is written.

# What to return
- `is_crisis`: your decision.
- `signal`: the shortest phrase from the message that shows risk or distress, copied verbatim; empty when `is_crisis` is false. If you cannot quote such a phrase, `is_crisis` must be false.
- `confidence`: 0 to 1.
