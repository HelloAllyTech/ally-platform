# Industries
- mental_health: therapy, counselling, psychiatry, psychology, de-addiction, emotional wellbeing services
- gp_clinic: doctors, clinics, hospitals, dentists, physiotherapy, any medical care
- diagnostic_lab: pathology labs, diagnostic and imaging centres, health check-ups
- pharmacy: pharmacies and chemists, online or in a store
- wellness: yoga, fitness, spa, nutrition coaching that is not medical care
- d2c: a brand selling products directly to shoppers
- coaching: tuition, test prep and coaching classes, mostly for students
- school: schools, preschools, colleges
- real_estate: builders, brokers, property listings
- saas: software products and their help centres
- restaurant: restaurants, cafés, cloud kitchens, catering
- legal_financial: lawyers, chartered accountants, financial advisers, lenders, insurance agents
- non_profit: charities, foundations, NGOs, trusts and campaigns, whatever their cause
- other: anything else

# Care topics
List every one of these that the business's work or information deals with, in `care_topics`:
- mental_health: mental health, emotional wellbeing, distress, suicide prevention
- medical: illness, symptoms, treatment, tests or hospitals
- medicines: medicines and prescriptions
- young_people: many of the people it serves are under 18
- legal_financial: advice on someone's own legal case or money

# Rules
1. Choose one industry. A non-profit is non_profit whatever its cause; put its cause in `care_topics` (a mental health foundation is non_profit with mental_health).
2. If a business (not a non-profit) offers any mental health service, choose mental_health, even if it does other things too.
3. If it offers medical care, tests or medicines, choose the health industry that fits best over any non-health one.
4. If many of the people it serves are under 18, choose coaching or school over a non-health industry.
5. List a care topic only when the business's own work deals with it, not because a page mentions it in passing.
6. `confidence` is between 0 and 1.
