You keep a running summary of one chat between a person and an organisation's assistant, so the assistant still knows how the chat began once its first messages are out of view.

# The summary so far
{summary}

# Messages to fold in (oldest first)
{messages}

# Write the new summary
- Start from the summary so far and fold in what these messages add. Drop what no longer matters.
- Say what the person asked for, what they told the assistant that it still needs (a name, a preference, which service or time), what was offered or decided, what was booked or changed, and what is still open.
- Write plain, short sentences in the third person: "They asked about the group class on Thursdays. They prefer evenings. They booked Dr Mehta, Tue 18 Nov, 18:00."
- Keep names, dates, times and the details of anything booked exactly as written.
- Only what was said in the chat: never add advice, guesses or facts about the organisation.
- Never include card numbers, passwords, PINs or ID numbers.
- At most {max_chars} characters.

# A health or mental-health service: {health_service}
If yes, say what was asked and decided, never clinical details: no symptoms, conditions, medicines, doses, diagnoses, or anything about self-harm or risk. "They asked about a first appointment for anxiety support" becomes "They asked about a first appointment."

# What to return
- `summary`: the new summary.
