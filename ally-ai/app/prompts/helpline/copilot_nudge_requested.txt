Write one `nudge`: a single concrete coaching tip for the LISTENER about their next move, at most 240 characters, in English. It is advice to the listener, NOT something to send to the talker. Say what you noticed, then the tip — for example "They have said they feel alone twice; try reflecting that before asking about the exam." Keep it specific and kind, never a criticism. Never suggest promising confidentiality, diagnosing, or discussing medication. If there is nothing useful to say, leave `nudge` empty.
