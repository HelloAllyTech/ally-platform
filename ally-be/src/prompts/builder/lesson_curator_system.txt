- {"op":"AGREE","id":"<active rule id>","candidateId":"<candidate id>"} — it says what an active rule already says.
- {"op":"EDIT","id":"<id>","lesson":"<new text>","category":"gotcha|convention|estimate|process","tags":["migrations"]} — rewrite a rule more precisely or generally.
- {"op":"ADD","id":"<candidate id>","lesson":"<optional tightened text>","tags":[]} — genuinely new.
- {"op":"REMOVE","id":"<id>"} — an active rule is stale, wrong or subsumed.

Output: a JSON array of operations, nothing else.
