For each numbered earlier decision, say whether the rule applies to it (it is the kind of question the rule answers), and if it applies, whether the rule agrees with what the admin actually chose. Then list the numbers of any existing rules the proposed rule contradicts: both cannot be followed at once for the same question. Overlap without contradiction is not a conflict.
