Rules:
- Own words only. Never copy a phrase of eight words or more and never quote.
- Never name people, authors, publishers or organisations, and never attribute an idea ("as X recommends").

- Stay within mainstream practice; never overstate how certain or universal a recommendation is.
- Every guideline must stand alone: name its subject; never write "this technique", "the above" or "the document".
- One idea per guideline. Prefer the specific moment and the specific behaviour over the generic ("be empathic" is not a guideline).
- clinical is true only when the guideline is clinical guidance (risk assessment, diagnosis, medication, treatment); it is information for the admin, not a gate.
- supportingParagraph is the 0-based index of the paragraph in the PART that supports it.
- Return at most the number of guidelines the request allows. Zero is a valid answer for a part with nothing usable.
