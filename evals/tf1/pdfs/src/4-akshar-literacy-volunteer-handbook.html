Akshar Literacy Foundation — Staff and volunteer handbook

Fictional organisation written for testing (TF1). Not a real service. For Akshar staff and volunteers only.

Akshar runs after-school reading classes for children aged 6 to 12 in 14 community centres in Lucknow. This handbook is for staff and volunteers.

Your first week

  • Day 1: induction at the head office, 11 am, with the volunteer coordinator, Mr. Rohit Srivastava.
  • Bring a copy of your ID and the signed code of conduct.
  • Day 2 to 5: shadow an experienced tutor at your assigned centre.
  • Your police verification must be submitted before you teach alone. HR helps you apply; it takes about 3 weeks.

Teaching

  • Classes run Monday to Friday, 4 pm to 6 pm. Each tutor has a group of at most 8 children.
  • We use the "Padho Saath" levelled readers, levels 1 to 6. Test each child's level in the first week of every month.
  • Record attendance in the Akshar app before 6:15 pm each day.

Leave and absence

Volunteers commit to at least 2 days a week for 6 months. If you can't come, tell your centre lead by 12 noon the same day. Staff get 18 days of paid leave a year, applied for in the HR portal.

Expenses

Travel is reimbursed at ₹4 per km for volunteers, up to ₹1,200 a month. Submit claims by the 5th of the next month with the expense form.

Safeguarding (child protection)

  • Never be alone with a child in a closed room. Keep doors open and stay within sight of another adult.
  • Do not share personal phone numbers or social media with children, or take photos of children on personal phones.
  • No physical punishment of any kind.
  • If a child tells you about abuse, or you suspect it: listen, do not promise to keep it secret, do not investigate, and report it to the Designated Safeguarding Lead, Ms. Farah Naqvi, on +91 522 400 9911 the same day. If the child is in immediate danger, call 112 and then Childline 1098.
  • Write down what the child said in their own words, with the date and time, and give it only to the Safeguarding Lead.
