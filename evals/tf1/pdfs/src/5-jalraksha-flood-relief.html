Jalraksha Flood Relief — Current updates and information

Fictional organisation written for testing (TF1). Not a real service.

Jalraksha runs relief camps, food distribution and health checks for families affected by the floods in Darbhanga district, Bihar.

Current updates

Update 1 — Relief camp moved (from 22 November). The relief camp at Bahadurpur Middle School has moved to the Laheriasarai Town Hall, near Laheriasarai railway station, because the school reopens for classes. Families registered at Bahadurpur keep the same registration card. Free tempo transport runs from Bahadurpur school gate to the Town Hall every 2 hours from 8 am to 6 pm until 30 November.

Update 2 — Donation drive extended to 15 December. The winter blanket and dry ration drive, which was to end on 30 November, is extended to 15 December. We still need 2,000 blankets. Collection point: Jalraksha office, Lalbagh, Darbhanga, 9 am to 5 pm, Monday to Saturday.

Relief camp services

  • Cooked meals three times a day: 8 am, 1 pm and 7 pm.
  • Free health check by a doctor every day, 10 am to 2 pm.
  • Drinking water, ORS packets and bleaching powder.
  • Bring your registration card, or your Aadhaar card to register for the first time.

Dry ration kits

Each registered family gets one kit every 15 days: 10 kg rice, 5 kg flour, 2 kg dal, 1 litre oil, salt and candles. Distribution at the camp, 3 pm to 5 pm.

How to donate

Bank transfer: Jalraksha Foundation, account 3366558899, IFSC SBIN0004321, SBI Darbhanga. Donations are eligible for 50% deduction under Section 80G. We do not collect cash door to door; anyone asking for cash in Jalraksha's name is not from us.

Contact

Helpdesk: +91 6272 245 600 (8 am to 8 pm). Office: Lalbagh, Darbhanga 846004. For rescue from flooded areas call the State Disaster Response Force through 112.
