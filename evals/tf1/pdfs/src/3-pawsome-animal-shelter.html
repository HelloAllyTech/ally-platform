Pawsome Animal Shelter — Supporters' guide

Fictional organisation written for testing (TF1). Not a real service.

Pawsome Animal Shelter rescues, treats and rehomes street dogs and cats in Bengaluru. We care for about 180 animals at our shelter in Hennur.

Ways to give

  • Bank transfer: Pawsome Animal Trust, account 50200011223344, IFSC HDFC0001234, HDFC Bank, Hennur branch.
  • UPI: pawsome@upi-example
  • Cheque: payable to "Pawsome Animal Trust", posted to the shelter address.
  • In kind: dry dog food, old bedsheets and newspapers, dropped at the shelter gate any day 9 am to 6 pm.

₹1,500 feeds one dog for a month. ₹3,000 pays for one sterilisation surgery.

Tax exemption (80G)

Donations of money to Pawsome Animal Trust are eligible for a 50% tax deduction under Section 80G. Our 80G registration number is AABTP1234Q24BL01. To get an 80G receipt, email your name, PAN, amount and transaction reference to donate@pawsome-blr.example within 30 days. Receipts are emailed within 10 working days. Cash donations above ₹2,000 are not eligible for 80G. Food and other in-kind gifts are not eligible.

Adoption

Adoption is free, but we ask for a ₹500 contribution towards vaccination. Steps: send an adoption enquiry, a home visit by our team, then a meet-and-greet at the shelter. Adopters must be 21 or older and have the landlord's permission if renting. We do not rehome animals outside Bengaluru.
