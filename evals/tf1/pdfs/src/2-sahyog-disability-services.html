Sahyog Disability Services — Services guide

Fictional organisation written for testing (TF1). Not a real service.

Sahyog helps people with disabilities and their families in Jaipur district get disability certificates, aids and appliances, and government benefits. All our services are free.

Our services

ServiceWho it is forWhat to bring
Help with a disability certificate (UDID card)Anyone with a disability of 40% or more, any ageAadhaar card, two passport photos, any doctor's reports
Aids and appliances (wheelchairs, hearing aids, crutches, white canes)People with a UDID card and family income under ₹22,500 a monthUDID card, income certificate, Aadhaar card
Help applying for the disability pensionAdults 18+ with 40% or more disabilityUDID card, bank passbook, Aadhaar card
Physiotherapy for childrenChildren under 14 with cerebral palsy or a movement disabilityAny doctor's reports

Sahyog helps you apply. The government medical board decides the disability percentage and who gets a certificate or pension; Sahyog does not decide eligibility.

Applying through us

You can start an application by giving your name, phone number, the service you need and the centre you prefer. A caseworker calls you within 3 working days to fix an appointment. You can have up to three open applications at a time.
