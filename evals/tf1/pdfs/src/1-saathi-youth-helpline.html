Saathi Youth Helpline — Information for callers

Fictional organisation written for testing (TF1). Not a real service.

Saathi Youth Helpline is a free, confidential listening service for young people aged 13 to 25 in Pune and across Maharashtra. Trained volunteer listeners answer calls and chats. We are not a medical service and we do not diagnose or prescribe.

When we are open

Every day, 10 am to 10 pm, including Sundays and public holidays. We are closed on Diwali day (Lakshmi Puja) only.

Helpline number: +91 20 4860 2525. WhatsApp chat on the same number. Calls are free from any network.

Callback requests

If you can't talk now, you can ask for a callback. Tell us your first name, a phone number and a good time between 10 am and 10 pm. A listener calls back within 4 hours during opening hours. Requests made after 9 pm are called back the next morning from 10 am. You don't have to tell us why you are calling.

The caller ID will show "Saathi". We let the phone ring twice and do not leave voicemail with details, to protect your privacy.

What happens on a call

  • A listener introduces themselves by first name only.
  • Calls usually last 20 to 40 minutes. There is no time limit.
  • You can stay anonymous. We do not record calls.
  • Languages: English, Hindi and Marathi.

What we don't do

  • We don't offer therapy, medicines or diagnosis. We can share a list of low-cost counsellors in Pune.
  • We don't contact your parents or school unless your life is in immediate danger.
  • We are not an emergency service. In an emergency call 112.
