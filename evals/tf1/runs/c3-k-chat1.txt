"You:
If I donate dog food, do I get 80G?

Pawsome Animal Shelter:
No — in-kind donations such as dog food are not eligible for an 80G tax deduction. Money donations are eligible for a 50% deduction under Section 80G (registration AABTP1234Q24BL01). To get an 80G receipt for a money donation, email your name, PAN, amount and transaction reference to donate@pawsome-blr.example within 30 days; receipts are emailed within 10 working days (team details as above).
