# Staff Handbook — Programme and Support Staff

> **Synthetic test document.** Written for Ally's use-case suite (O4, staff and volunteer onboarding). It is not Teach For India's handbook and does not describe its real policies. Names, numbers and addresses are invented.

Version 3.2 · Effective 1 April 2026 · Owner: People team (Priya Menon, people@tfi-handbook.test)

## 1. Welcome and how to use this handbook

This handbook is for full-time programme and support staff in all city offices. It does not apply to Fellows, whose terms are in the Fellowship Agreement, or to volunteers, who follow the Volunteer Guide.

If this handbook doesn't answer your question, ask your manager first, then the People team at people@tfi-handbook.test. The People team replies within two working days.

Where this handbook and your appointment letter differ, your appointment letter wins.

## 2. Working hours and attendance

- Standard working hours are **9:30 am to 6:00 pm, Monday to Friday**, with a one-hour lunch break.
- Programme staff who visit schools may start at **8:00 am** on school days and finish at **4:30 pm**.
- The second and fourth **Saturdays** are working days for programme staff during the school term (for school events and Fellow support). Support staff do not work Saturdays.
- **Flexible hours:** with your manager's approval you may start any time between 8:00 am and 10:30 am, as long as you work 8 hours.
- **Working from home:** up to **2 days a week**, agreed with your manager in advance. Not during the first 30 days of employment.
- Attendance is marked in the HR portal (Keka) by 11:00 am each day.

## 3. Leave

The leave year runs from **1 April to 31 March**. Leave is applied for in Keka and approved by your manager.

| Type | Days per year | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Earned leave | 18 | Accrues 1.5 days a month. Up to **10 days** carry forward to the next year; the rest lapses. Apply at least **7 days** ahead for 3 or more days |
| Casual leave | 8 | For short, unplanned needs. At most 3 days in a row. Does not carry forward |
| Sick leave | 10 | A doctor's certificate is needed for **more than 2 days in a row**. Does not carry forward |
| Public holidays | 12 | The list for your city is published in Keka every March |
| Floating holidays | 2 | Any festival or personal day of your choice |

- **Maternity leave:** 26 weeks of paid leave, as the law provides, for employees who have worked at least 80 days in the 12 months before the expected date of delivery.
- **Paternity leave:** 10 working days, taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption.
- **Adoption leave:** 12 weeks for an adopting mother of a child under 3 months.
- **Bereavement leave:** 5 working days on the death of an immediate family member (parent, spouse, child, sibling, parent-in-law).
- **Leave during probation:** you may take casual and sick leave during probation; earned leave can be taken after probation ends, though it accrues from your first day.
- **Unpaid leave:** up to 30 days in a year with your manager's and the People team's approval.
- **Leave encashment:** earned leave is not encashed while you are employed. On leaving, up to 30 days of earned leave are paid out.

## 4. Probation and confirmation

- Probation is **6 months** from your joining date.
- Your manager reviews you at 3 months and at 6 months. On a good review you are confirmed in writing.
- Probation may be extended once, by up to 3 months.
- During probation the notice period is **15 days** on either side.

## 5. Notice period and leaving

- After confirmation the notice period is **60 days** for staff at Manager level and above, and **30 days** for everyone else.
- Resign by email to your manager, copying people@tfi-handbook.test.
- Your full and final settlement is paid within **45 days** of your last working day, after you return your laptop, ID card and any advances are settled.

## 6. Pay, payslips and benefits

- Salaries are paid on the **last working day of each month** by bank transfer.
- Payslips are in Keka by the 3rd of the next month.
- Salary details are confidential. The handbook does not list salaries; questions about your own pay go to the People team.
- **Health insurance:** a group medical cover of **₹5 lakh a year** for you, your spouse and up to two children, from your first day. Parents can be added at your own cost (about ₹14,000 a year for two parents).
- **Term life cover:** three times your annual fixed pay.
- **Provident Fund:** 12% of basic pay from you and 12% from the organisation.
- **Employee Assistance Programme:** free, confidential counselling for you and your family, 24 hours a day: call 1800 000 4321 (a test number) or book at eap.tfi-handbook.test. Up to 6 sessions a year per person.

## 7. Reimbursements and travel

Claims are submitted in Keka **within 30 days** of spending, with bills. Approved claims are paid with the next salary.

| What | Limit |
|---|---|
| Local travel for work (auto, cab, metro) | Actual cost; cabs only when public transport isn't practical. Own two-wheeler ₹4 per km, own car ₹9 per km |
| Mobile and internet | Up to **₹1,000 a month** |
| Meals when working past 8 pm or on a Saturday | Up to **₹300** per day |
| Outstation travel | Train 3AC or economy air, booked through the Admin team at least 7 days ahead |
| Outstation hotel | Up to ₹3,500 a night in metro cities, ₹2,500 elsewhere |
| Daily allowance outstation | ₹800 a day |
| Learning and development | Up to **₹15,000 a year** for courses or books related to your role, approved in advance by your manager |

Not reimbursed: alcohol, fines, personal entertainment, travel between home and your usual office.

**Advances** for outstation travel can be requested 5 working days before travel and must be settled within 7 days of returning.

## 8. Code of conduct

- Treat children, families, Fellows, schools and colleagues with respect at all times.
- **Child protection:** never be alone with a child behind a closed door; never share personal contact details with students; never post photos of students on personal social media. Report any concern to the Child Protection Officer (Anita Rao, cpo@tfi-handbook.test) within 24 hours.
- **Gifts:** you may accept gifts worth up to ₹1,000 from partners; anything more is declined politely or declared to your manager.
- **Conflicts of interest:** declare any relationship with a vendor, school or partner to your manager in writing.
- **Social media:** don't speak for the organisation without the Communications team's approval.
- **Confidentiality:** student data, donor data and salaries are confidential. Don't take them off approved systems.
- Breaches can lead to a warning, suspension or dismissal, after an inquiry.

## 9. POSH: prevention of sexual harassment

- The organisation has zero tolerance for sexual harassment, in line with the POSH Act, 2013.
- An **Internal Committee (IC)** handles complaints. Presiding Officer: Meera Iyer. External member: Adv. Kavita Shah. Write to ic@tfi-handbook.test.
- A complaint should be made in writing within **3 months** of the incident (the IC can extend this by another 3 months for good reason).
- The IC completes its inquiry within **90 days**. Complaints are confidential, and retaliation against anyone who complains or gives evidence is itself misconduct.
- Everyone completes the online POSH training within their first 30 days and every year after.

## 10. IT and devices

- You get a **laptop** on your first day. It remains the organisation's property and is returned when you leave.
- Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT (it@tfi-handbook.test) and your manager **within 24 hours**, and file a police report.
- Your email is firstname.lastname@tfi-handbook.test. Turn on two-step verification in your first week.
- Use only approved tools for student data (the School Data Portal and Google Workspace). Never store student data on personal devices or USB drives.
- IT support: raise a ticket at help.tfi-handbook.test, or call extension 4040 between 9 am and 6 pm on weekdays.
- Wi-Fi password for guests is shared at reception; staff use the "TFI-Staff" network with their email login.
