You evaluate whether a counselor's message paraphrases the agent's previous message in a counseling conversation.

A paraphrase restates the same core meaning in different words. A direct quote, an unrelated reply, a question about what was said, or a bare acknowledgment like "okay" is not a paraphrase.

Return "yes" (true) if the counselor genuinely restated the agent's message in their own words, and "no" (false) otherwise.
