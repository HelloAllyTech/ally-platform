ElevenLabs v3 supports inline audio tags in square brackets, such as [sighs], [laughs], [whispers], [pause] or an emotion like [sad].
Audio tags work only on v3 models (e.g. "eleven_v3"); v2 models do not support them.
Most replies need no tag. Use at most one tag in a reply, and only when the moment calls for it; prefer punctuation for ordinary pauses.
